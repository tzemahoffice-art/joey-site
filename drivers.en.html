Joining

Four steps to your first ride.

  1. Sign up

    Name and phone number in the driver app, with an SMS code.

  2. Choose your vehicle

    Taxi or van, and your licence classes.

  3. Upload documents

    A photo, ID, driving licence, vehicle licence and a vehicle photo.

  4. Get approved

    The Joey team checks your documents; once approved you can go online and receive offers.

What you need

Before you start.

  • The right licence

    A driving licence in the class for your vehicle and for carrying passengers.

  • The right vehicle

    A licensed taxi, or a van for 8, 13 or 16 passengers.

  • A smartphone

    With location on while you are online.

What you get

The tools for the job.

  • Offers to your phone

    A new offer arrives as a notification with a sound — you see how far the pickup is, and decide.

  • Know where before you accept

    The pickup address and how far it is are shown in the offer itself.

  • An emergency button for drivers too

    On every active ride, the same way as for riders — saved at once and sent to the team.

  • The rider pays in the app

    By card only — no cash, and no handling payment in the car.

Your location is shared only while you are online or on a ride — and stops when you go offline.
