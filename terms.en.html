2. What Joey is

Joey is a platform that connects riders and delivery senders with independent, licensed vehicle operators. Joey does not itself provide transportation or delivery services, and is not a taxi or delivery company — it operates the technology that lets riders and drivers find and transact with each other. Drivers on Joey are independent operators, not Joey employees or agents. [This positioning is an intentional legal/regulatory choice already reflected in the project's design (see CLAUDE.md §8 — Joey is described internally as "a connection venue between a rider and a licensed vehicle owner," with license-to-vehicle-type matching handled through the driver approval process, not through pricing logic). A lawyer should review and confirm this framing is legally sound before publication — it is not a decision this document is making on its own.]

Joey offers two services, selectable in the Joey Rider app:

  • Ride — passenger transportation, in one of five vehicle types (standard car, van, premium, XL, or motorbike)

  • Delivery — package delivery, in the same set of vehicle types

5. Pricing and payment

Israeli law requires a licensed taxi to charge fares based on its official physical meter, not an app-generated price. This affects who sets the price, not how it's charged — Joey always charges your saved payment card automatically, for every vehicle type. There is no cash payment, and you never pay a driver directly.

  • For taxi (car) rides: the price shown in the app before and during the ride is an estimate only, for your planning purposes. The actual fare is set by the driver's physical meter and entered into the app by the driver — Joey then charges your saved payment card that exact amount automatically.

  • For all other vehicle types (van, premium, XL, motorbike) and for deliveries: Joey calculates the final price itself and charges it automatically to your saved payment card when the ride or delivery is completed.

  • In every case, there is no manual "pay" step and no cash payment.

  • A valid saved payment method is required to request most rides (this requirement can vary in the future by country, as reflected in the app's own configuration). Joey does not process or store your full card number — see the Privacy Policy §2.4 for exactly how card data is handled through our payment processor, PayPlus.

  • Prices are set according to Joey's published pricing (base fare, per-kilometer rate, per-minute rate, which may vary by vehicle type and country) at the time of your request, subject to change without prior individual notice.

12. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your account at any time, as described in the Privacy Policy §7 — a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel, and your phone number released for future reuse once it completes.

When a deletion request cannot be made. The request will be refused while any of the following is true, and the app will show you which one is blocking it:

  • you have an active ride (as rider or driver) — finish or cancel it first;

  • you have an unpaid positive wallet balance;

  • you have an open debt owed to the platform.

What is kept after deletion. Your ride and payment history is retained and detached from your name, phone and email — but the content itself is kept in full, including the pickup and drop-off addresses of your rides, the full text of your chat messages, and the rating comments you wrote. The precise list, and the reasons for it, are in Privacy Policy §7.4 — read it before submitting a deletion request.
