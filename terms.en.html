2. What Joey is

Joey is a platform that connects riders and delivery senders with independent, licensed vehicle operators. Joey does not itself provide transportation or delivery services, and is not a taxi or delivery company — it operates the technology that lets riders and drivers find and transact with each other. Drivers on Joey are independent operators, not Joey employees or agents.

Joey offers two services, selectable in the Joey Rider app:

  • Ride — passenger transportation by taxi or van. A passenger ride is never made on a motorbike or any other two-wheeled vehicle.

  • Delivery — package delivery. The vehicle depends on the package size you choose, and a delivery may also be made by motorbike or by a micromobility vehicle (such as an e-scooter or an e-bike).

3. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create a Joey account. By using Joey, you confirm that the information you provide during registration is accurate.

4. Accounts

  • Joey accounts are created and accessed using your phone number and a one-time SMS verification code — there is no password.

  • You are responsible for keeping access to your phone number secure, since it is how your account is authenticated.

  • One phone number corresponds to one account. The same account can be both a rider and a driver. A second account cannot be opened with the same number: registering with a number we already have signs you in to your existing account.

  • Riders must have a valid saved payment method on file before requesting most rides (see §7) — this is enforced by the app itself, not just a policy statement.

5. Pricing and payment

Israeli law requires a licensed taxi to charge fares based on its official physical meter, not an app-generated price. This affects who sets the price, not how it's charged — Joey always charges your saved payment card automatically, for every vehicle type. There is no cash payment, and you never pay a driver directly.

  • For taxi (car) rides: the price shown in the app before and during the ride is an estimate only, for your planning purposes. The actual fare is set by the driver's physical meter and entered into the app by the driver — Joey then charges your saved payment card that exact amount automatically.

  • For van rides and for deliveries: Joey calculates the final price itself and charges it automatically to your saved payment card when the ride or delivery is completed.

  • In every case, there is no manual "pay" step and no cash payment.

  • A valid saved payment method is required to request most rides (this requirement can vary in the future by country, as reflected in the app's own configuration). Joey does not process or store your full card number — see the Privacy Policy §2.4 for exactly how card data is handled through our payment processor, PayPlus.

  • A charge that did not go through remains your debt. If the charge for a ride, a delivery or a cancellation fee does not go through (for example, the card is declined), the amount remains a debt on your account with no time limit, and you can pay it at any time from “Payment methods” in the app. Until the debt is paid in full, you cannot book a ride or a delivery. Joey does not delete debts.

  • Prices are set according to Joey's published pricing (base fare, per-kilometer rate, per-minute rate, which may vary by vehicle type and country) at the time of your request, subject to change without prior individual notice.

6. Cancellations and cancellation fees

  • Cancelling after that window may incur a cancellation fee (currently ₪15).

  • Drivers may also cancel a ride; repeated driver-side or rider-side cancellations may affect your ability to use Joey (see §10).

8. Driver eligibility, documents, and approval

If you register as a driver:

  • You must provide accurate vehicle information and upload identity and vehicle documents (ID, driving licence, selfie, vehicle photo) as part of onboarding.

  • Your account is reviewed and must be approved before you can accept rides. Joey may reject an application, and may request updated documents at any time.

  • You are solely responsible for holding any license, permit, or insurance required by law to operate your vehicle for the service type you register for. Joey's driver approval process checks that your submitted documents match your registered vehicle type, but approval by Joey does not substitute for your own legal responsibility to be properly licensed and insured.

  • Joey may suspend or terminate a driver's ability to accept rides, with or without a stated reason related to safety, fraud, policy violations, or repeated complaints. A suspended driver's active ride at the time of suspension is unaffected — suspension only prevents new ride assignments going forward.

  • For every ride and delivery, including taxi rides, Joey collects payment automatically from the rider's saved card. There is no cash collection and no direct collection from the rider, for any vehicle type. For taxi rides, the amount charged is exactly what you entered from the physical meter; for every other vehicle type and for deliveries, Joey calculates the amount itself.

  • You are settled once a month, based on a monthly report. Money accrues in your favour during the month, Joey's commission is deducted from it at the end of the month rather than on each ride, and the remainder is paid to you by bank transfer. There is no self-service withdrawal: every payment goes through Joey's bookkeeping.

10. Ratings

Riders and drivers may rate each other after a completed ride. Ratings are used to maintain service quality and may factor into account review decisions.

11. In-app communication and safety features

  • Sending messages to your matched driver or rider is possible while the ride is active. After the ride ends: the rider can write to the driver for up to 24 hours (for example about an item left in the car). The driver cannot start a conversation after the ride — only reply to a rider who wrote, within the same 24 hours. After that the conversation is read-only; for anything else, contact Joey support.

  • Reading the conversation: a rider sees the conversations of their rides with no time limit; for a driver, a ride's conversation is shown for 90 days after it ends. Message content is also retained after account deletion — see Privacy Policy §7.5.

  • The in-app SOS feature sends your location and a timestamp to Joey when activated. It is not a substitute for contacting emergency services (police, ambulance) directly, and Joey does not guarantee any particular response time.

12. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your account at any time, as described in the Privacy Policy §7 — a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel, and your phone number released for future reuse once it completes.

When a deletion request cannot be made. The request will be refused while any of the following is true, and the app will show you which one is blocking it:

  • you have an active ride (as rider or driver) — finish or cancel it first;

  • (drivers) you have a month whose settlement is still open — money owed to you or by you that has not been settled yet. In that case, contact Joey support, who will close the settlement;

  • you have an unpaid charge (a ride, a delivery or a cancellation fee whose charge did not go through) — pay it first, from “Payment methods” in the app. If such a charge appears during the 30-day grace period, the deletion is put on hold until it is paid.

What is kept after deletion. Your ride and payment history is retained and detached from your name, phone and email — but the content itself is kept in full, including the pickup and drop-off addresses of your rides, the full text of your chat messages, and the rating comments you wrote. The precise list, and the reasons for it, are in Privacy Policy §7.5 — read it before submitting a deletion request.
