Services

Three ways to get there.

  • Taxi

    Licensed taxis, up to 4 passengers. The fare is set by the meter under the price-control order; an estimate is shown before you book.

  • Group van

    Vans for 8, 13 and 16 passengers. A fixed price up to 15 km, shown before you book — and that is the price you pay.

  • Delivery

    A small, medium or large package. The price is final and shown up front, and only the courier delivering it sees the recipient’s details.

How it works

From pickup to destination.

  1. Choose where to

    A pickup and a destination — or one of your recent addresses.

  2. See the route and price

    On the map, for each vehicle or package size, before you book.

  3. An approved driver accepts

    And you watch them approach on the map in real time, with their name and rating.

  4. Pay and rate

    Your saved card is charged at the end, and both sides rate each other.

Safety

Built for a safe ride.

SOS

An emergency button — for the rider and for the driver

On every active ride, one tap. The alert is saved at once — with the location, when known — and reaches the Joey team — even if the app closes a second after the tap, it is already with us.

  • Checked drivers

    Every driver uploads an ID, a driving licence, a vehicle licence and a vehicle photo, and gets rides only after the Joey team approves.

  • Contact during the ride

    Call or chat with the driver. Phone numbers are reachable only while the ride is active.

  • Card only

    No cash. Every charge is clear and listed in your trip history.

Drivers

Driving? Join Joey.

Taxi drivers and van operators with the right licence: sign up in the driver app, upload your documents, and once approved, ride offers come straight to your phone.
