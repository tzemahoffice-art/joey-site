Rides and deliveries, in one app

Book a taxi, a van for a group or a courier for a package — an approved driver nearby takes the request. You see the price before you book, and pay by card in the app.

For drivers Contact us

Services

  • Taxi

    Licensed taxis, up to 4 passengers. The fare is set by the taxi meter under the price-control order; an estimate is shown before you book.

  • Group van

    Vans for 8, 13 and 16 passengers. A fixed price up to 15 km, shown before you book — and that is the price you pay.

  • Delivery

    A small, medium or large package, from ₪30. The price is final and shown up front. Only the courier delivering it sees the recipient’s details.

How it works

  1. Choose where to

    A pickup and a destination — or one from your trip history.

  2. Choose a vehicle or package size

    and see the price and the route on the map before you book.

  3. An approved driver accepts

    and you watch them approach in real time, with name, vehicle and rating.

  4. Pay and rate

    Your saved card is charged at the end, and both sides rate each other.

Safety and trust

  • Checked drivers

    Every driver uploads an ID, a driving licence and a vehicle licence, and gets rides only after approval.

  • Emergency button

    On every active ride — for the rider and for the driver.

  • Contact during the ride

    Call or chat with the driver. Phone numbers are reachable only while the ride is active.

  • Card only

    No cash — every charge is clear and listed in your trip history.

For drivers

Taxi drivers and van operators with the right licence can join Joey: sign up in the driver app, choose your vehicle type, upload your documents — and once approved, start receiving requests from riders and senders nearby. The rider pays through the app.

Questions about joining: support@joey-go.com
