Effective date: 22/11/2026 Last updated: 25/11/2026

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information every account provides

  • Full name

  • Phone number (used to sign in — Joey uses phone number + one-time SMS code, not a password)

  • Email address — drivers are required to give one (a copy of the driver agreement and notices are sent there). For riders it is optional: an optional field at sign-up, for receipts by email.

  • Preferred language

  • Country

2.2 If you're a rider

  • Pickup and drop-off addresses and precise GPS coordinates for each ride you request

  • Your ride history: service type (ride or delivery), vehicle type requested, status, prices, and timestamps

  • Chat messages you exchange with your driver — can be sent during the ride and for up to 24 hours after it ends, and you can still read them after the ride (see Terms of Service §11)

  • Ratings you give to drivers and receive from them

  • Emergency contacts you choose to add (name and phone number), if you use that feature

  • Your location and timestamp if you use the in-app SOS button

  • A device push-notification token, so we can send you ride-status notifications

  • A promo code, if you enter one

2.3 If you're a driver

Everything in 2.1 and 2.2 that applies to a driver role, plus:

  • Vehicle information: type, plate number, model

  • Your ID number, and the identity and vehicle documents you upload during onboarding (ID card photo and a face photo for verification, driving licence, vehicle licence, insurance, vehicle photo) — stored in a private file storage bucket, never publicly accessible; only accessed through short-lived, individually-generated links

  • Your live location while you are online in the app or on an active ride (see §3, "Location," below for exactly how this works today)

  • The amounts accrued in your favour and your monthly settlement statements

  • Payment and settlement details — listed in section 2.3a below

  • Your driving/service rating

2.3a If you're a driver — payment and settlement details

We collect these details to pay you each month, to issue the invoices and receipts, and to withhold tax at source as the law requires. For each item: who sees it, whether it is encrypted, and how long it is kept. "The Joey team" here means everyone who works in Joey's management system; "the company's owner" means the owner only.

  • Bank account details — bank, branch and account number. Who sees them: in the app you see only the last 4 digits; in the Joey team — only the company's owner. Encrypted: stored encrypted, and the encryption key is not in the database. How long: as long as your account exists; deleted when your account is deleted.

  • The account holder's name. Used to make sure the payment goes to an account in your name. Who sees it: you and the company's owner. Not encrypted. Deleted when your account is deleted.

  • A withholding-tax exemption or reduction certificate that you upload (the photo and its expiry date). Who sees it: you and the company's owner. Not encrypted; kept in private storage. Deleted when your account is deleted.

  • Your withholding-tax rate and its validity, as the company's owner checks and records it with the Tax Authority. Who sees it: you and the Joey team; only the company's owner records it. Not encrypted. Kept after your account is deleted.

  • Business (VAT dealer) number. Who sees it: you and the Joey team. Not encrypted. Kept after your account is deleted.

  • VAT status (licensed dealer or exempt dealer). Who sees it: you and the Joey team. Not encrypted. Kept after your account is deleted.

  • Invoice address and e-mail. Who sees them: you and the Joey team. Not encrypted. Deleted when your account is deleted.

  • Business certificate and bank-account confirmation (the photos you upload). Who sees them: you; the management system shows the Joey team only whether they were uploaded. Not encrypted; kept in private storage. Deleted when your account is deleted.

  • The records of payments to you — the transfers, the gross amount, the tax withheld at source and the rate it was withheld at, the monthly statements, and the receipts and invoices you send to Joey. Who sees them: you see your monthly statement; the Joey team sees all of them. Not encrypted. Kept for at least 7 years from the end of the tax year, also after your account is deleted, and they cannot be deleted before then.

No rider sees any of these details (see §5).

2.4 Payment information — what we do not store

When you add a payment card, card entry happens on PayPlus's own secure payment page — not inside the Joey app, and not on Joey's servers. Joey never receives, sees, or stores your full card number or CVV. What we do store, after PayPlus confirms your card is added, is:

  • The last 4 digits of your card and the card brand (e.g., "Visa •••• 1234"), so you can recognize which card is saved

  • The card's expiry month and year, when PayPlus sends them, so we can show you when the card expires

  • PayPlus's own reference tokens for that card, which we use to ask PayPlus to charge you — these tokens are meaningless outside PayPlus's own system

2.5 Information we generate automatically

  • Ride/trip records, prices, and transaction records (see §6, "Payments," for how pricing works)

  • Records of driver-approval decisions and the reasons for them

  • Basic app usage/error logs

2.6 If you send a delivery — the recipient's details

When you book a delivery, you give us the name and phone number of the person receiving the package, so the courier can hand it over. That person has not signed up to Joey, has not agreed to this policy, and has no way to ask us to delete anything themselves — so we treat their details differently from yours:

  • Use: only to deliver that package. The name and phone number are shown to the courier carrying it out and are used for nothing else — no marketing, no later contact.

  • Automatic deletion after 30 days: 30 days after the delivery is completed or cancelled, the recipient's name and phone number are deleted from the record. The delivery record itself (addresses, price, times) is kept like any ride — only the recipient's details are removed. The 30 days exist so that a delivery that went wrong can be looked into.

  • This deletion does not depend on your account — it happens whether your account stays open or you delete it.

  • Before you give us someone else's details, make sure they are expecting the delivery and do not object to the courier having their phone number.

2.7 A photo of the package, when the courier reports it is bigger than declared

If the courier arrives for pickup and finds the package is bigger than the size that was chosen, they must photograph it before reporting it. The photo has one purpose: so you can see what it is about before you approve a new price or decline, and so we can look into a complaint if there is one.

  • Who sees it: you (the sender), the courier who took it, and Joey staff when handling an inquiry or a complaint. It is shown to no one else and used for nothing else.

  • What it may show: a photo of a package may show a label with a name and address — yours or the recipient's. We ask couriers to photograph the package only.

  • Deletion after 180 days: 180 days after the delivery is completed or cancelled, the photo is deleted automatically. The 180 days exist so that a delivery that went wrong can be looked into, including a card chargeback, whose window is longer than 30 days.

2.8 Requests to the Joey team (in-app support chat)

When you write to us through "Chat with us" in the app, we keep your messages, the team's replies, and when each message was sent.

  • Who sees it: you, and the Joey staff who handle requests. Staff may also see your recent rides, so they can answer a request about a ride or a charge. We record internally which staff member replied, but do not show you their details.

  • Use: only to handle your request — not for marketing.

  • Notification: when the team replies, you get a notification on your phone. It does not include the text of the reply, because notifications are also shown on a locked screen.

  • Deletion: a support conversation that was closed is deleted 24 months after it was closed, even while the account is active; and all your support conversations are deleted outright when you delete your account (see §7). This is different from ride chat with a driver or rider, which is kept — a support conversation has no other party who needs the record.

2.9 Cookies, local storage and tracking tools

Checked in practice on 08/11/2026. We use no marketing, advertising or analytics tools — not on the website and not in the apps. That is also why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing that needs your consent.

  • The website joey-go.com sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser's storage, and loads no scripts — neither ours nor anyone else's. It has no external fonts, embedded frames or pixels. The website's hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log requests on its own servers, such as the IP address, to run and secure the service. This is not tracking by Joey and does not use cookies.

  • The apps (rider and driver) include no analytics, advertising or tracking library of our own. The maps in the apps are Google Maps, and Google's maps library collects technical data itself — according to Google: crash reports and performance data, map interactions (panning and zooming), device details (model and system version), the IP address and an identifier of the library — to maintain and improve Google's services. Joey does not receive this data and does not use it. They keep on your device only what they need to work: your signed-in session, the language you chose, your biometric sign-in preference, a discount code you entered, and small display states (for example whether you have already seen a reply from the Joey team). This stays on the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

  • If this changes: an analytics or marketing tool will not load before you agree to it, and you will be able to refuse — and this section will be updated before it is switched on. The same applies to Cloudflare's "I'm not a robot" check before an SMS code is sent, which is not switched on today.

3. Location — how it actually works today

Riders: your location is used to set your pickup point and to show your driver's live location while a ride is in progress. It is not tracked when you're not actively planning or on a ride.

Drivers: while you are online (available for ride requests) or on an active ride, the app records your current GPS location so we can match you with nearby ride requests and so a rider on an active ride can see your live position. While you are online and waiting for an offer, this location is stored as a current-state value only — the database keeps only your most recent location, not a historical trail of everywhere you've been. On an active ride (from when you accept it until it is completed or cancelled) the route of the ride is also kept — at most one location point every 10 seconds — as evidence of the route you drove (see §8). Riders can only see a driver's location during a ride they are actually matched to.

Also while the app is in the background — only after you go online, and always visibly. While you are online or on an active ride, your location keeps being sent even when the driver app is in the background (for example while you use a navigation app, or with the screen off) — so you keep receiving offers, and a rider on an active ride keeps seeing you. It starts only when you yourself tap "Go online", and for as long as it runs a permanent indicator is shown: on Android, a permanent "Joey — you are online" notification; on iPhone, the blue location indicator at the top of the screen. Sharing stops when you tap "Go offline", and when you close the app completely. We do not ask for "Always" location permission, and we do not collect location while you are offline. While the app is in the background, your location is sent about once a minute while you wait for an offer, and about every 15 seconds on an active ride (more often while the app is open on screen). In the background too, only your latest location is kept while you wait; on an active ride the route of the ride is also kept, as described above.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: matching riders and drivers, navigation, calculating trip prices, processing payments, and enabling in-ride communication

  • To verify driver identity and eligibility to drive (document review, approval workflow)

  • To send you ride-status and account notifications

  • To respond to support requests

  • To investigate safety incidents (e.g., an SOS alert) and cooperate with emergency services where appropriate

  • To maintain financial and transaction records for accounting and legal purposes (see §8, "How long we keep your information")

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers ("processors") that are actually necessary to run Joey, and only the information each one genuinely needs to perform its function:

  • Supabase — hosts our entire database and file storage. Effectively all app data passes through Supabase's infrastructure. The Supabase servers where the data is stored are in the European Union (Paris, France).

  • Twilio — delivers the one-time SMS codes used to sign in. Twilio sees your phone number and the fact that a code was sent to it.

  • PayPlus — processes payments, as described in §2.4. PayPlus sees whatever information is needed to process a charge (amount, and the card details you enter directly on PayPlus's own page — never through Joey).

  • Google — (1) Joey Rider uses Google's address-autocomplete service (Places API) to help you search for pickup/drop-off addresses as you type; the text you type into the address search is sent to Google for that purpose. (2) When you set an address from your current location or by moving a pin on the map, the coordinates are sent to Google to find the address. (3) The pickup and destination are sent to Google (Routes API) to calculate the route and the price. (4) The maps in the apps are Google Maps. Joey Driver does not use the address search.

  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your phone (through Apple and Google). It receives your device's push token and the text of the notification; for a chat message, that includes the sender's first name and the beginning of the message.

  • Resend — sends Joey's emails: to drivers (for example a copy of the driver agreement and notices of changes), and to the Joey team — about a support request (without its content and without a name or phone number), and about an SOS call (with the caller's name, phone number and location).

  • Pushover — when an SOS call is made, it alerts Joey's owner on his phone, with the caller's name and phone number and a link to the location.

  • Render — hosts the Joey team's management system (servers in Frankfurt, Germany); information the team views passes through it.

Between the two parties of a ride we share only what is needed for the ride to happen — no less and no more:

  • The rider sees about the driver: first name, vehicle type and model, licence plate number, the driver's average rating, and the driver's live location during the ride.

  • The driver sees about the rider: first name, the pickup and destination addresses, and — in the ride offer and during the ride — the rider's average rating and the number of ratings it is based on.

  • Phone numbers — each side's number is visible to the other only while the ride is active (see below).

  • Chat messages — as set out in the Terms of Service §11.

Neither side sees the other's last name, the addresses saved in their account (such as "Home" and "Work", or a driver's billing address), their documents, email, ride history or payment details. The only addresses the driver sees are the pickup and destination of the ride itself.

Phone numbers — mutually visible during an active ride only. So that a rider and driver can coordinate by phone (for example when a rider cannot find the car), each one's phone number is visible to the other — but only while the ride is active (from the moment the driver accepts until the ride is completed or cancelled). Once the ride closes, access to the number is automatically blocked at the database level.

The call itself is placed through your device's own phone app, not through Joey. This means the call will appear in both devices' call history, and the number will remain there after the ride has ended — that is outside our control, exactly as with any ordinary phone call.

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a genuine safety emergency.

6. Payments — how pricing and charging work

Israeli law requires that a licensed taxi charge fares based on its official physical meter, not an app estimate. Because of this, who sets the price differs by vehicle type — but how it's charged does not: in every case, Joey charges your saved payment card automatically when the trip is completed. There is no cash payment, and you are never asked to pay the driver directly, for any vehicle type.

  • For taxi (car) rides, the price you see in the app before and during the ride is an estimate only. The final amount is set by the driver's physical meter, entered into the app by the driver — and the app then charges your saved payment card that exact amount automatically; if you used a promo code, the discount comes off the meter amount.

  • For every other vehicle type, and for deliveries, the app calculates the final price itself and charges your saved payment card automatically when the trip is completed.

  • In every case: no manual "Pay" step, and no cash.

If you cancel a ride within a short free-cancellation window after a driver accepts (currently 3 minutes), there is no charge. After that window, a cancellation fee may apply (currently ₪15).

7. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Joey account at any time from Settings in the app. Here is exactly what happens:

  1. Requesting deletion starts a 30-day grace period. Your account is immediately locked — you can no longer request new rides — but nothing is deleted yet.

  2. You can cancel the deletion request at any time during those 30 days by signing back into the app and choosing to cancel. If you don't cancel, deletion proceeds automatically once the 30 days pass.

  3. An unpaid charge stops the deletion. You cannot request deletion while your account has an unpaid charge, and if such a charge appears during the 30 days, the deletion is put on hold — nothing is deleted — until it is paid. The reason: a debt is not deleted, and it has to stay linked to you until it is paid — for at most 7 years from the ride in which it arose. After 7 years the deletion goes ahead, and the debt remains only in the financial records, without name, phone or email. (See the Terms of Service §5, §12.)

  4. What is permanently removed once the 30 days pass:

    • Your name, phone number, email address, and profile photo.

    • Your saved payment card details, emergency contacts, and push-notification token — deleted outright.

    • The last device location we held for you.

    • All your support conversations with the Joey team — your messages and the team's. Unlike ride chat (item 4 below), support conversations are not kept (see §2.8).

    • If you are a driver: the photo of your ID card and the face photo used for verification are deleted already once your account is approved. Your ID number, driving licence, vehicle licence and insurance are kept for 7 years from your last ride, also after account deletion, to defend against claims, as evidence that we checked them, in a closed archive that only the company's owner can access. Your ID number is included in the daily database backup; the images of the documents themselves are not included in any backup today. The vehicle photo and your driving licence categories are deleted; your vehicle registration number and model are removed from your driver record. Of the payment and settlement details: the bank account details, the withholding-tax exemption certificate, the invoice address and e-mail, the business certificate and the bank-account confirmation are deleted; the business number, the VAT status and the withholding-tax rate are kept (see §2.3a).

  1. What is kept — and exactly what that includes. Your name, phone and email are removed from these records, which remain without name, phone or email. The content itself is kept, for the periods in section 8, including:

    • The pickup and drop-off addresses of every ride you took — as entered, including coordinates, subject to the deletion of Google content in section 8 (coordinates that came from Google are deleted, and in a taxi ride an address that is not a street address you picked in search is shortened to the city). If you used your home or work address, it may be kept — as text, as coordinates, or both.

    • The full text of the chat messages you sent and received.

    • The free-text comments you wrote in ratings.

    • Your payment and transaction records, and any SOS alerts you raised — including the location they were raised from.

    • Records of the consents and confirmations you gave — consent to receive invoices and receipts as computerized documents, acceptance of the Terms of Service, and for drivers also acceptance of the driver agreement and the insurance declaration: which version, and when.

    We keep these specifically because: (a) the other party to a ride (your driver or rider) has a legitimate record-keeping interest in that ride's history that shouldn't disappear because you deleted your account, and (b) we have financial and legal obligations to retain transaction records (see §8).

    🔴 Please understand: removing your name and phone number does not necessarily make this information impossible to trace back to you. A home address held in ride history, or the content of a chat message, can in practice point to a specific person even without a name. We say this plainly so you do not read "deletion" more broadly than it actually is.

  1. Your phone number is released once deletion completes — a different person can sign up with that number in the future, and their account starts completely empty; nothing from your old account carries over to them.

  2. This process is irreversible once the 30 days have passed.

8. How long we keep your information

  • While your account is active: for as long as you keep using Joey.

  • Trip, payment and transaction records: kept for at least 7 years from the end of the tax year in which they were created, including after account deletion. They are kept because tax law requires it, and for no other purpose. For a van ride or a delivery this includes the origin and destination addresses as text.

  • Location coordinates that came from Google (when you picked an address in search or from your recent addresses): deleted 30 days after the ride is completed or cancelled. Coordinates are kept only when they came from your phone's GPS or from a pin you moved on the map. At the same time, in a taxi ride, an address that is not a street address you picked in search is shortened to the city; in a van ride or a delivery the address text is kept. Google's place ID is kept.

  • The driver's GPS track of each ride: 7 years, as evidence of the route driven.

  • Ride chat messages: 24 months after the ride ended.

  • Text comments in ratings: 24 months after the rating was given (the rating's stars are kept).

  • A delivery recipient's name and phone number: 30 days after the delivery is completed or cancelled — then deleted automatically (see §2.6).

  • A package photo taken by the courier: 180 days after the delivery is completed or cancelled — then deleted automatically (see §2.7).

  • Support conversations with the Joey team: 24 months from when the conversation was closed — and deleted outright when the account is deleted (see §2.8, §7).

  • Driver documents: the ID card photo and the face photo — until the account is approved; the ID number, driving licence, vehicle licence and insurance — 7 years from the last ride, also after account deletion, to defend against claims. While the account is active, our operations team sees them to approve and renew, and you see your own; after account deletion — in a closed archive that only the company's owner can access (see §7).

  • Drivers' payment and settlement details: item by item — see §2.3a.

  • SOS alerts and safety-incident records: 7 years, including after account deletion, so that we can investigate a safety incident or complaint.

  • An unpaid debt: holds the account deletion for at most 7 years from the ride (see §7).

9. Your rights

You can:

  • Access and review the personal information we hold about you (contact us, or use the in-app profile screens where available)

  • Correct inaccurate information

  • Request deletion of your account (§7)

  • Withdraw consent for optional features (e.g., emergency contacts) by simply not using them, or removing what you've added

You may also file a complaint with the Privacy Protection Authority.

10. Children's privacy

Joey is intended for people aged 18 and over, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age.

11. Security

We use industry-standard technical measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, database-level access controls that restrict each user to their own data, and private (non-public) storage for sensitive documents accessed only via short-lived signed links. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
