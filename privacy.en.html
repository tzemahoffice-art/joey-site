Joey — Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: 10/11/2026 Last updated: 10/11/2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy covers both Joey mobile applications — Joey Rider and Joey Driver (together, "Joey," "we," "us," "our"). One policy covers both apps; sections that apply to only one app say so explicitly.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information every account provides

  • Full name

  • Phone number (used to sign in — Joey uses phone number + one-time SMS code, not a password)

  • Email address (optional)

  • Preferred language

  • Country

  • Profile photo, if you add one (Joey Rider does not currently have a working photo-upload feature; this may change in a future update)

2.2 If you're a rider

  • Pickup and drop-off addresses and precise GPS coordinates for each ride you request

  • Your ride history: service type (ride or delivery), vehicle type requested, status, prices, and timestamps

  • Chat messages you exchange with your driver — can be sent during the ride and for up to 24 hours after it ends, and you can still read them after the ride (see Terms of Service §11)

  • Ratings you give to drivers and receive from them

  • Emergency contacts you choose to add (name and phone number), if you use that feature

  • Your location and timestamp if you use the in-app SOS button

  • A device push-notification token, so we can send you ride-status notifications

  • A promo code, if you enter one

2.3 If you're a driver

Everything in 2.1 and 2.2 that applies to a driver role, plus:

  • Vehicle information: type, plate number, model

  • Identity and vehicle documents you upload during onboarding (ID card, driving license, vehicle photo) — stored in a private file storage bucket, never publicly accessible; only accessed through short-lived, individually-generated links

  • Your live location while you are online in the app or on an active ride (see §3, "Location," below for exactly how this works today)

  • The amounts accrued in your favour and your monthly settlement statements

  • Your driving/service rating

2.4 Payment information — what we do not store

When you add a payment card, card entry happens on PayPlus's own secure payment page — not inside the Joey app, and not on Joey's servers. Joey never receives, sees, or stores your full card number, expiry date, or CVV. What we do store, after PayPlus confirms your card is added, is:

  • The last 4 digits of your card and the card brand (e.g., "Visa •••• 1234"), so you can recognize which card is saved

  • PayPlus's own reference tokens for that card, which we use to ask PayPlus to charge you — these tokens are meaningless outside PayPlus's own system

2.5 Information we generate automatically

  • Ride/trip records, prices, and transaction records (see §7, "Payments," for how pricing works)

  • Records of driver-approval decisions and the reasons for them

  • Basic app usage/error logs

2.6 If you send a delivery — the recipient's details

When you book a delivery, you give us the name and phone number of the person receiving the package, so the courier can hand it over. That person has not signed up to Joey, has not agreed to this policy, and has no way to ask us to delete anything themselves — so we treat their details differently from yours:

  • Use: only to deliver that package. The name and phone number are shown to the courier carrying it out and are used for nothing else — no marketing, no later contact.

  • Automatic deletion after 30 days: 30 days after the delivery is completed or cancelled, the recipient's name and phone number are deleted from the record. The delivery record itself (addresses, price, times) is kept like any ride — only the recipient's details are removed. The 30 days exist so that a delivery that went wrong can be looked into.

  • This deletion does not depend on your account — it happens whether your account stays open or you delete it.

  • Before you give us someone else's details, make sure they are expecting the delivery and do not object to the courier having their phone number.

2.7 A photo of the package, when the courier reports it is bigger than declared

If the courier arrives for pickup and finds the package is bigger than the size that was chosen, they must photograph it before reporting it. The photo has one purpose: so you can see what it is about before you approve a new price or decline, and so we can look into a complaint if there is one.

  • Who sees it: you (the sender), the courier who took it, and Joey staff when handling an inquiry or a complaint. It is shown to no one else and used for nothing else.

  • What it may show: a photo of a package may show a label with a name and address — yours or the recipient's. We ask couriers to photograph the package only.

  • Deletion after 30 days: 30 days after the delivery is completed or cancelled, we begin deleting the photo and complete it without delay — for the same reason as the recipient's details (§2.6). The 30 days exist so that a delivery that went wrong can be looked into.

2.8 Requests to the Joey team (in-app support chat)

When you write to us through "Chat with us" in the app, we keep your messages, the team's replies, and when each message was sent.

  • Who sees it: you, and the Joey staff who handle requests. Staff may also see your recent rides, so they can answer a request about a ride or a charge. We record internally which staff member replied, but do not show you their details.

  • Use: only to handle your request — not for marketing.

  • Notification: when the team replies, you get a notification on your phone. It does not include the text of the reply, because notifications are also shown on a locked screen.

  • Deletion: all your support conversations are deleted outright when you delete your account (see §7). This is different from ride chat with a driver or rider, which is kept — a support conversation has no other party who needs the record.

2.9 Cookies, local storage and tracking tools

Checked in practice on 08/11/2026. We use no marketing, advertising or analytics tools — not on the website and not in the apps. That is also why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing that needs your consent.

  • The website joey-go.com sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser's storage, and loads no scripts — neither ours nor anyone else's. It has no external fonts, embedded frames or pixels. The website's hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log requests on its own servers, such as the IP address, to run and secure the service. This is not tracking by Joey and does not use cookies.

  • The apps (rider and driver) include no analytics, advertising or tracking library. They keep on your device only what they need to work: your signed-in session, the language you chose, your "remember me" and biometric sign-in preferences, a discount code you entered, and small display states (for example whether you have already seen a reply from the Joey team). This stays on the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

  • If this changes: an analytics or marketing tool will not load before you agree to it, and you will be able to refuse — and this section will be updated before it is switched on. The same applies to Cloudflare's "I'm not a robot" check before an SMS code is sent, which is not switched on today.

3. Location — how it actually works today

Riders: your location is used to set your pickup point and to show your driver's live location while a ride is in progress. It is not tracked when you're not actively planning or on a ride.

Drivers: while you are online (available for ride requests) or on an active ride, the app records your current GPS location so we can match you with nearby ride requests and so a rider on an active ride can see your live position. This location is stored as a current-state value only — the database keeps only your most recent location, not a historical trail of everywhere you've been. Riders can only see a driver's location during a ride they are actually matched to.

Also while the app is in the background — only after you go online, and always visibly. While you are online or on an active ride, your location keeps being sent even when the driver app is in the background (for example while you use a navigation app, or with the screen off) — so you keep receiving offers, and a rider on an active ride keeps seeing you. It starts only when you yourself tap "Go online", and for as long as it runs a permanent indicator is shown: on Android, a permanent "Joey — you are online" notification; on iPhone, the blue location indicator at the top of the screen. Sharing stops when you tap "Go offline", and when you close the app completely. We do not ask for "Always" location permission, and we do not collect location while you are offline. While waiting for an offer your location is sent about once a minute, and on an active ride about every 15 seconds — and in the background too, only your latest location is kept, not a route history.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: matching riders and drivers, navigation, calculating trip prices, processing payments, and enabling in-ride communication

  • To verify driver identity and eligibility to drive (document review, approval workflow)

  • To send you ride-status and account notifications

  • To respond to support requests

  • To investigate safety incidents (e.g., an SOS alert) and cooperate with emergency services where appropriate

  • To maintain financial and transaction records for accounting and legal purposes (see §8, "How long we keep your information")

  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers ("processors") that are actually necessary to run Joey, and only the information each one genuinely needs to perform its function:

  • Supabase — hosts our entire database and file storage. Effectively all app data passes through Supabase's infrastructure. The Supabase servers where the data is stored are in the European Union (Paris, France).

  • Twilio — delivers the one-time SMS codes used to sign in. Twilio sees your phone number and the fact that a code was sent to it.

  • PayPlus — processes payments, as described in §2.4. PayPlus sees whatever information is needed to process a charge (amount, and the card details you enter directly on PayPlus's own page — never through Joey).

  • Google (Places API) — Joey Rider uses Google's address-autocomplete service to help you search for pickup/drop-off addresses as you type. The text you type into the address search is sent to Google for that purpose. Joey Driver does not use this feature.

  • Map rendering uses your device's built-in map framework (e.g., Apple Maps on iOS), subject to that platform's own data practices, not a separate data-sharing arrangement we've made with a mapping provider.

Between the two parties of a ride we share only what is needed for the ride to happen — no less and no more:

  • The rider sees about the driver: first name, vehicle type and model, licence plate number, the driver's average rating, and the driver's live location during the ride.

  • The driver sees about the rider: first name, the pickup and destination addresses, and — in the ride offer — the rider's average rating and number of rides.

  • Phone numbers — each side's number is visible to the other only while the ride is active (see below).

  • Chat messages — as set out in the Terms of Service §11.

Neither side sees the other's last name, the addresses saved in their account (such as "Home" and "Work", or a driver's billing address), their documents, email, ride history or payment details. The only addresses the driver sees are the pickup and destination of the ride itself.

Phone numbers — mutually visible during an active ride only. So that a rider and driver can coordinate by phone (for example when a rider cannot find the car), each one's phone number is visible to the other — but only while the ride is active (from the moment the driver accepts until the ride is completed or cancelled). Once the ride closes, access to the number is automatically blocked at the database level.

The call itself is placed through your device's own phone app, not through Joey. This means the call will appear in both devices' call history, and the number will remain there after the ride has ended — that is outside our control, exactly as with any ordinary phone call.

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a genuine safety emergency.

6. Payments — how pricing and charging work

Israeli law requires that a licensed taxi charge fares based on its official physical meter, not an app estimate. Because of this, who sets the price differs by vehicle type — but how it's charged does not: in every case, Joey charges your saved payment card automatically when the trip is completed. There is no cash payment, and you are never asked to pay the driver directly, for any vehicle type.

  • For taxi (car) rides, the price you see in the app before and during the ride is an estimate only. The final amount is set by the driver's physical meter, entered into the app by the driver — and the app then charges your saved payment card that exact amount automatically.

  • For every other vehicle type, and for deliveries, the app calculates the final price itself and charges your saved payment card automatically when the trip is completed.

  • In every case: no manual "Pay" step, and no cash.

If you cancel a ride within a short free-cancellation window after a driver accepts (currently 3 minutes), there is no charge. After that window, a cancellation fee may apply (currently ₪15).

7. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Joey account at any time from Settings in the app. Here is exactly what happens:

  1. Requesting deletion starts a 30-day grace period. Your account is immediately locked — you can no longer request new rides — but nothing is deleted yet.

  2. You can cancel the deletion request at any time during those 30 days by signing back into the app and choosing to cancel. If you don't cancel, deletion proceeds automatically once the 30 days pass.

  3. An unpaid charge stops the deletion. You cannot request deletion while your account has an unpaid charge, and if such a charge appears during the 30 days, the deletion is put on hold — nothing is deleted — until it is paid. The reason: a debt is not deleted, and it has to stay linked to you until it is paid. (See the Terms of Service §5, §12.)

  4. What is permanently removed once the 30 days pass:

    • Your name, phone number, email address, and profile photo.

    • Your saved payment card details, emergency contacts, and push-notification token — deleted outright.

    • The last device location we held for you.

    • All your support conversations with the Joey team — your messages and the team's. Unlike ride chat (item 4 below), support conversations are not kept (see §2.8).

    • If you are a driver: the documents you uploaded (ID card, driving licence, selfie, vehicle photo) are deleted from storage; your driving licence categories are deleted; and your vehicle registration number and model are removed from your driver record.

  1. What is kept — and exactly what that includes. Your identifying details (name, phone, email) are removed from these records, which remain linked only to an anonymous internal reference. But the content itself is kept in full, including:

    • The pickup and drop-off addresses of every ride you took — exactly as entered, including coordinates. If you used your home or work address, it is kept.

    • The full text of the chat messages you sent and received.

    • The free-text comments you wrote in ratings.

    • Your payment and transaction records, and any SOS alerts you raised — including the location they were raised from.

    We keep these specifically because: (a) the other party to a ride (your driver or rider) has a legitimate record-keeping interest in that ride's history that shouldn't disappear because you deleted your account, and (b) we have financial and legal obligations to retain transaction records (see §8).

    🔴 Please understand: removing your name and phone number does not necessarily make this information impossible to trace back to you. A home address held in ride history, or the content of a chat message, can in practice point to a specific person even without a name. We say this plainly so you do not read "deletion" more broadly than it actually is.

  1. Your phone number is released once deletion completes — a different person can sign up with that number in the future, and their account starts completely empty; nothing from your old account carries over to them.

  2. This process is irreversible once the 30 days have passed.

8. How long we keep your information

  • While your account is active: for as long as you keep using Joey.

  • Financial data and ride records (rides, charges, receipts, invoices and monthly statements): 7 years, as required by the Israeli book-keeping rules — 7 years from the end of the tax year, or 6 years from filing that year's return, whichever is later. They are kept even after an account is deleted (see §7), and are not deleted before then.

  • A delivery recipient's name and phone number: 30 days after the delivery is completed or cancelled — then deleted automatically (see §2.6).

  • A package photo taken by the courier: 30 days after the delivery is completed or cancelled — then we begin deleting it and complete it without delay (see §2.7).

  • Support conversations with the Joey team: for as long as the account exists — and deleted outright when the account is deleted (see §2.8, §7).

  • Identity documents (drivers): for as long as the account exists; deleted when the account is deleted (see §7).

  • SOS alerts and safety-incident records: kept even after account deletion, so that we can investigate a safety incident or complaint.

9. Your rights

You can:

  • Access and review the personal information we hold about you (contact us, or use the in-app profile screens where available)

  • Correct inaccurate information

  • Request deletion of your account (§7)

  • Withdraw consent for optional features (e.g., emergency contacts) by simply not using them, or removing what you've added

You may also file a complaint with the Privacy Protection Authority.

10. Children's privacy

Joey is intended for people aged 18 and over, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age.

11. Security

We use industry-standard technical measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, database-level access controls that restrict each user to their own data, and private (non-public) storage for sensitive documents accessed only via short-lived signed links. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
