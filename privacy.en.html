2. Information we collect

2.1 Information every account provides

  • Full name

  • Phone number (used to sign in — Joey uses phone number + one-time SMS code, not a password)

  • Email address (optional)

  • Preferred language

  • Country

  • Profile photo, if you add one (Joey Rider does not currently have a working photo-upload feature; this may change in a future update)

2.2 If you're a rider

  • Pickup and drop-off addresses and precise GPS coordinates for each ride you request

  • Your ride history: service type (ride or delivery), vehicle type requested, status, prices, and timestamps

  • Chat messages you exchange with your driver — only visible while a ride is in the accepted/arriving/in_progress stages; not accessible before or after

  • Ratings you give to drivers and receive from them

  • Emergency contacts you choose to add (name and phone number), if you use that feature

  • Your location and timestamp if you use the in-app SOS button

  • A device push-notification token, so we can send you ride-status notifications

  • A promo code, if you enter one

2.3 If you're a driver

Everything in 2.1 and 2.2 that applies to a driver role, plus:

  • Vehicle information: type, plate number, model

  • Identity and vehicle documents you upload during onboarding (ID card, driving license, vehicle photo) — stored in a private file storage bucket, never publicly accessible; only accessed through short-lived, individually-generated links

  • Your live location while you are online in the app or on an active ride (see §3, "Location," below for exactly how this works today)

  • Your wallet balance (accumulated earnings)

  • Your driving/service rating

2.4 Payment information — what we do not store

When you add a payment card, card entry happens on PayPlus's own secure payment page — not inside the Joey app, and not on Joey's servers. Joey never receives, sees, or stores your full card number, expiry date, or CVV. What we do store, after PayPlus confirms your card is added, is:

  • The last 4 digits of your card and the card brand (e.g., "Visa •••• 1234"), so you can recognize which card is saved

  • PayPlus's own reference tokens for that card, which we use to ask PayPlus to charge you — these tokens are meaningless outside PayPlus's own system

2.5 Information we generate automatically

  • Ride/trip records, prices, and transaction records (see §7, "Payments," for how pricing works)

  • Records of driver-approval decisions and the reasons for them

  • Basic app usage/error logs

3. Location — how it actually works today

Riders: your location is used to set your pickup point and to show your driver's live location while a ride is in progress. It is not tracked when you're not actively planning or on a ride.

Drivers: while you are online (available for ride requests) or on an active ride, the app records your current GPS location so we can match you with nearby ride requests and so a rider on an active ride can see your live position. This location is stored as a current-state value only — the database keeps only your most recent location, not a historical trail of everywhere you've been. Riders can only see a driver's location during a ride they are actually matched to.

Location tracking today only happens while the app is open and in the foreground. Joey does not currently track location while the driver app is closed or backgrounded. 4. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: matching riders and drivers, navigation, calculating trip prices, processing payments, and enabling in-ride communication

  • To verify driver identity and eligibility to drive (document review, approval workflow)

  • To send you ride-status and account notifications

  • To respond to support requests

  • To investigate safety incidents (e.g., an SOS alert) and cooperate with emergency services where appropriate

  • To maintain financial and transaction records for accounting and legal purposes (see §8, "How long we keep your information")

  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers ("processors") that are actually necessary to run Joey, and only the information each one genuinely needs to perform its function:

  • Supabase — hosts our entire database and file storage. Effectively all app data passes through Supabase's infrastructure. [VERIFY: which region/country Supabase's infrastructure for this project is hosted in, for the purposes of any required international-transfer disclosure.]

  • Twilio — delivers the one-time SMS codes used to sign in. Twilio sees your phone number and the fact that a code was sent to it.

  • PayPlus — processes payments, as described in §2.4. PayPlus sees whatever information is needed to process a charge (amount, and the card details you enter directly on PayPlus's own page — never through Joey).

  • Google (Places API) — Joey Rider uses Google's address-autocomplete service to help you search for pickup/drop-off addresses as you type. The text you type into the address search is sent to Google for that purpose. Joey Driver does not use this feature.

  • Map rendering uses your device's built-in map framework (e.g., Apple Maps on iOS), subject to that platform's own data practices, not a separate data-sharing arrangement we've made with a mapping provider.

We also share limited information between the two parties of a ride: a rider and their matched driver can see each other's name, profile photo (if any), and — only while the ride is active — chat messages and live location, so the ride can actually happen. This is the minimum needed to complete a ride safely, not a general information exchange.

Phone numbers — mutually visible during an active ride only. So that a rider and driver can coordinate by phone (for example when a rider cannot find the car), each one's phone number is visible to the other — but only while the ride is active (from the moment the driver accepts until the ride is completed or cancelled). Once the ride closes, access to the number is automatically blocked at the database level.

The call itself is placed through your device's own phone app, not through Joey. This means the call will appear in both devices' call history, and the number will remain there after the ride has ended — that is outside our control, exactly as with any ordinary phone call. 6. Payments — how pricing and charging work

Israeli law requires that a licensed taxi charge fares based on its official physical meter, not an app estimate. Because of this, who sets the price differs by vehicle type — but how it's charged does not: in every case, Joey charges your saved payment card automatically when the trip is completed. There is no cash payment, and you are never asked to pay the driver directly, for any vehicle type.

  • For taxi (car) rides, the price you see in the app before and during the ride is an estimate only. The final amount is set by the driver's physical meter, entered into the app by the driver — and the app then charges your saved payment card that exact amount automatically.

  • For every other vehicle type, and for deliveries, the app calculates the final price itself and charges your saved payment card automatically when the trip is completed.

  • In every case: no manual "Pay" step, and no cash.

If you cancel a ride within a short free-cancellation window after a driver accepts (currently 3 minutes), there is no charge. After that window, a cancellation fee may apply (currently [₪15 for Israel — confirm this is still the current value in app_settings before publishing, as it can change]).

7. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your Joey account at any time from Settings in the app. Here is exactly what happens:

  1. Requesting deletion starts a 30-day grace period. Your account is immediately locked — you can no longer request new rides — but nothing is deleted yet.

  2. You can cancel the deletion request at any time during those 30 days by signing back into the app and choosing to cancel. If you don't cancel, deletion proceeds automatically once the 30 days pass.

  3. What is permanently removed once the 30 days pass:

    • Your name, phone number, email address, and profile photo.

    • Your saved payment card details, emergency contacts, and push-notification token — deleted outright.

    • The last device location we held for you.

    • If you are a driver: the documents you uploaded (ID card, driving licence, selfie, vehicle photo) are deleted from storage; your driving licence categories are deleted; and your vehicle registration number and model are removed from your driver record.

    [LAWYER / JOEY OWNERS: if this document commits to a fixed window for completing deletion (e.g. "within 30 days"), confirm with the technical team that deletion of driver document files runs automatically rather than being triggered by hand — as at the date of this draft the mechanism exists and is tested, but is triggered manually]

  4. What is kept — and exactly what that includes. Your identifying details (name, phone, email) are removed from these records, which remain linked only to an anonymous internal reference. But the content itself is kept in full, including:

    • The pickup and drop-off addresses of every ride you took — exactly as entered, including coordinates. If you used your home or work address, it is kept.

    • The full text of the chat messages you sent and received.

    • The free-text comments you wrote in ratings.

    • Your payment and transaction records, and any SOS alerts you raised — including the location they were raised from.

    We keep these specifically because: (a) the other party to a ride (your driver or rider) has a legitimate record-keeping interest in that ride's history that shouldn't disappear because you deleted your account, and (b) we have financial and legal obligations to retain transaction records (see §8).

    🔴 Please understand: removing your name and phone number does not necessarily make this information impossible to trace back to you. A home address held in ride history, or the content of a chat message, can in practice point to a specific person even without a name. We say this plainly so you do not read "deletion" more broadly than it actually is.

  5. Your phone number is released once deletion completes — a different person can sign up with that number in the future, and their account starts completely empty; nothing from your old account carries over to them.

  6. This process is irreversible once the 30 days have passed.
