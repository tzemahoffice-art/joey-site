Joey — Accessibility Statement (English)

This statement covers the website joey-go.com only. The accessibility of the Joey Rider and Joey Driver apps has not been assessed and is not covered here — see "What is not yet accessible" below.

Last updated: 10/11/2026

1. Our commitment

Y.A.R. Zemach Holdings Ltd. treats accessibility as part of the service rather than an addition to it. We want anyone to be able to read the documents on this site, whatever their ability, disability, or assistive technology.

2. The standard this site was made accessible to

This site was made accessible in accordance with Israeli Standard SI 5568, and the W3C's WCAG 2.1 guidelines, at conformance level AA.

This follows the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Accessibility Adjustments for Services) Regulations, 5773-2013.

3. What was checked and made accessible

The following was actually tested on 31/10/2026, across every page of the site in both languages: the home page, the drivers page, the privacy policy, the terms of service and this statement.

  • Colour contrast — every text-and-background combination on the site was measured by calculation, not judged by eye, including semi-transparent backgrounds against what is actually behind them. Every pair meets the requirement: at least 4.5:1 for normal text and at least 3:1 for large text and for graphical elements (icons, the focus indicator). On the home and drivers pages the weakest text pair is 5.17:1 and the focus indicator 3.66:1; on the document pages, 4.83:1. The check is re-run after every colour change (scripts/audit-site-contrast.js).

  • Hierarchical heading structure — every page begins with a single h1, and heading levels descend in order without skipping.

  • Full keyboard navigation — the whole site is operable by keyboard. There is no interactive component anywhere on it other than links, there are no focus traps, and everything that can receive focus has a visible focus indicator — a bold yellow outline.

  • Skip link — every page starts with a "Skip to main content" link, revealed on keyboard focus, so the repeated header can be bypassed.

  • Language marking — every page carries correct lang and dir attributes, and on the bilingual home page each language block is marked separately with its own language and direction. The links that switch language are marked in the language they lead to.

  • Text alternatives for images — every image that carries information (the app screenshots, the driver’s offer notification, the background photo) has alternative text describing it. Purely decorative graphics — the route line, the section dividers and the icons beside headings — are marked so that screen readers skip them.

  • Motion — nothing on the site moves by itself. A visitor who has set "reduce motion" on their device does not see even the small hover transitions.

  • Text resizing — text can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content or functionality (the menu moves to a row of its own), and pinch-zoom is not blocked on phones. Sizes are set in relative units, not fixed heights.

  • Phone display — checked at phone width, with no horizontal scrolling — including at 320 pixels (a phone with enlarged text), where horizontal scrolling in the top bar of the English pages was found and fixed on 08/11/2026.

  • Automatic check on every update — since 08/11/2026, every change to the site is checked automatically before it is published: WCAG 2.1 level A and AA rules (the axe tool), a keyboard pass over every page checking the focus indicator, display at 200% and at 320 pixels without horizontal scrolling, alternative text on every image, and the contrast of every text and background pair. A change that fails is not published.

  • Semantic structure — pages are built from real content landmarks (header, nav, main, article, footer), real lists and real blockquotes, rather than from visual styling alone.

  • Valid markup — the site has no duplicate IDs and no malformed tags.

Where the site was tested

  • Operating system and browser: Windows 11, Google Chrome (version 156) — automated check (axe, WCAG 2.1 A+AA) and a keyboard pass, at desktop width (1280), at 200% (640) and at phone width (320). The same check runs in Chrome on Linux on every update of the site.

  • Screen readers: not yet tested by hand.

  • Other browsers: Safari, Firefox and Edge have not been tested yet.

4. Privacy: this site has no cookies and no tracking

This belongs here rather than in a separate notice, because it means there is no overlay, dialog or banner to dismiss before you can read anything.

Verified on 31/10/2026, again on 08/11/2026 — and since then automatically on every update: the site contains no scripts at all, no third-party scripts, no external fonts, no embedded frames, and no use of cookies or local storage. Images are hosted on the site itself — nothing is loaded from any other server. Fonts are the device's own system fonts. The site sets no cookies and collects no visitor information — which is also why there is no cookie banner, since one would state something untrue.

The site's hosting provider may log requests on its own server (an IP address, for instance), as any web server does. That is not tracking by Joey and is not cookie-based.

5. What is not yet accessible — exceptions

We state the exceptions plainly rather than leaving them to be inferred:

  • 🔴 This site has not been surveyed by an external certified accessibility expert. The testing in section 3 was carried out internally against SI 5568 / WCAG 2.1 AA.

  • There are no accessible PDF versions of the documents. They are meant to be read in a browser, and are styled for printing directly from it (including saving as PDF) without losing content.

  • 🔴 The Joey Rider and Joey Driver apps are not covered by this statement. Their accessibility has not been assessed. A separate accessibility statement for the Joey apps will be published when the service launches.

  • Anyone who needs an individual accommodation can contact the accessibility coordinator, and we will help.
