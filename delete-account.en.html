Delete your Joey account

This page is for users of the Joey (riders) and Joey Driver (drivers) apps, by Y.A.R. Zemach Holdings Ltd.

The quick way — in the app

Open the app → Settings → Delete account, and confirm.

Without the app — in writing

If you no longer have the app (for example, you removed it or changed phones), write to support@joey-go.com with:

  • Subject: "Account deletion request"

  • The phone number you signed up with

  • Whether it is a rider account, a driver account, or both

We verify the request against the phone number registered to the account before we open it. A request that was not verified is not handled, so nobody can delete someone else's account.

We open a verified request within 7 business days of receiving it, and email you when it is open.

What happens after the request

  1. 30 days to change your mind. The account is locked at once — no new rides or offers — but nothing is deleted yet. Within the 30 days you can cancel: sign in to the app and choose to cancel.

  2. After 30 days — deletion. Permanently removed: your name, phone number and email address; your saved card, emergency contacts and notification details; your support conversations. For drivers also: your licence classes, your plate number and vehicle model, and your bank account details. (The ID card photo and the face photo are already deleted when the account is approved.)

  3. What stays — without your name or phone number: ride history (including pickup and drop-off addresses, subject to the shortening in section 8 of the Privacy Policy), ride chat messages, comments in ratings, SOS alerts, the consents and acceptances you gave, and the financial records the law requires us to keep (at least 7 years from the end of the tax year). For drivers, also kept: the GPS track of each ride (7 years), and the ID number, driving licence, vehicle licence and insurance — 7 years from the last ride, to defend against claims, in an archive that only the company owner can open. The full details are in sections 7 and 8 of the Privacy Policy.

When deletion has to wait

  • An active ride — until it ends.

  • An unpaid charge — a debt is not deleted; once it is paid, you can delete.

  • Drivers: an open settlement month — until we transfer what you are owed for the month, or a debt is settled.

For an active ride and an open settlement month the app tells you what is in the way. In any case you can reach us in the in-app support chat or at support@joey-go.com.
